    Four kinds of training

    Part-147 maintenance

    Basic modules and type courses for B1 and B2 licences, from organisations holding a Part-147 approval. Each listing shows its approval reference, the aircraft types it teaches and the language of instruction.

    A320 · A330 · A350 · B737 · B787 · ATR · ERJ

    CAMO, Part-M and ARC

    Courses for continuing airworthiness staff: nominated persons, airworthiness review staff, planners and records teams. Part-CAMO and Part-ML compliance, AMP development, AD and SB tracking.

    Part-CAMO · Part-ML · Airworthiness review · Reliability

    Lease transition and OIL

    Delivery and redelivery, mid-lease and pre-purchase inspections, records audits back to birth. Learn to run an Open Item List that both lessor and lessee sign off, item by item, until the aircraft changes hands.

    Return conditions · LLP back-to-birth · Records digitisation · OIL

    Pilot and cabin crew

    Approved training organisations (ATOs) for licences, type ratings and cabin crew attestation. Integrated and modular ATPL, MCC, UPRT, business-jet type ratings and recurrent safety training.

    ATPL · Type rating · MCC/JOC · Cabin crew attestation

    Why this directory exists

    For eighteen years I have worked on the airworthiness side of aviation: CAMO management, airworthiness reviews, and standing on hangar floors for lessors while an aircraft changed hands.

    Before that I taught Part-M, Part-145 and avionics courses for six years. The question I heard most from students, and later from operators, was never about the regulation. It was simpler: where can I take this course, from someone approved to teach it?

    The answer was scattered across authority registers, PDFs and word of mouth, country by country. So this directory puts it on one map, with the approval reference beside every name.

    It is part of a larger platform I am building for lessors, lessees, operators and CAMOs, with an Open Item List for deliveries and redeliveries at its centre. Training comes first, because every good transition starts with people who know what they are looking at.

    Questions, answered

    Who is this for? Engineers and mechanics looking for licence or type courses, CAMO and records staff, lessor and lessee technical teams, pilots and cabin crew, and the managers who send them on training.

    Are the organisations really approved? Each listing shows the approval reference the organisation gave us, so you can check it on the authority's own register before you enrol. Providers that teach without an approval, such as lease transition seminars, are marked as training providers.

    What does the approval reference mean? It is the number on the organisation's certificate: the country code, the regulation and a serial, for example AT.147.0031 for a Part-147 school in Austria.

    Does it cost anything to search? No. Searching the map and writing to a school is free, and you deal with the school directly.

    Why does the plane leave from Vienna? That is where the platform is based. The route is only for fun; courses are open to students from anywhere the school accepts.

    A country I need is not highlighted. It means no organisation there is listed yet. Tell us which one you are looking for and we will look for schools there first.

    Can my organisation be listed? Yes. Send your approval certificate, the courses you run and the languages you teach in, and we add you after checking the reference.

    Are online courses included? Yes. Every listing says whether it teaches in the classroom, online, in a simulator or on site.

    What is the Open Item List? The running list of findings that lessor and lessee agree to close before an aircraft is delivered or returned. The lease transition courses here teach how to build and close one.

    Is this an authority website? No. Techlog is an independent platform. The authorities' registers remain the official source.
